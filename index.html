Frequently asked questions

Anything else, our concierge line is staffed around the clock.

Do you actually own every vehicle?

Yes — every car in the collection is titled to Junction Motors. We never sublease from third-party owners or other rental agencies.

What's the minimum age to reserve?

25 for most of the collection, 30 for our top-tier exotics. A valid license and proof of insurance are required at delivery.

Can I extend my reservation mid-rental?

Yes, subject to availability — message your concierge and we'll confirm within the hour, often without you needing to swap vehicles.

Is a security deposit required?

A refundable hold is placed on file at delivery, released automatically within 5 business days of a clean return.

Do you deliver outside your three hub cities?

On request — long-distance delivery is available for an additional fee, most commonly arranged for weddings and corporate events.
